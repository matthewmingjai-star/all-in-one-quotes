01 · Analysis

Driving Environment Diagnosis & Our Solutions

Your Environment & Key Concerns

  • Highway stone chips: A large MPV has a tall, upright front. At 80–100 km/h, flying stones hit hard, and conventional PVC wrap films can be punctured, damaging the paint underneath.
  • Acid rain & water spots: Parked outdoors half the time, the micro-textured matte surface can easily trap water spots after rain and strong sun. Low-grade films with weak UV resistance may also yellow or fade early.
  • Silver edges & lifting corners: Going from light silver to deep midnight blue, any panel gaps not wrapped deep enough will show silver edges when the doors open. High-pressure washing can also lift corners.

Our Professional Approach

  • 8.0 mil optical-grade coloured TPU: A tougher, thicker cushioning layer than conventional wrap films, with a heat-activated self-healing top coat that absorbs stone impacts and greatly reduces the risk of paint damage.
  • Nano fluorocarbon hydrophobic coating: A lotus-effect surface helps rain and dust roll off more easily. Combined with a weather-resistant aliphatic base, it keeps the matte midnight blue deep and even.
  • Precision deep-edge wrapping: Headlights, trims and door edges are wrapped deep into the panel inner edges with a dedicated edge primer, for a clean finish with less lifting under pressure washing.
